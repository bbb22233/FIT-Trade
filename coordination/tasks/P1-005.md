## Objective

Deliver durable internal events without making NATS authoritative, and make
duplicate, delayed, wrong-owner, failed, and unknown delivery states observable.

## Deliverables

- Outbox publisher with lease, retry, bounded backoff, and crash recovery
- Authenticated JetStream streams, subjects, consumers, retention, and
  dead-letter policy for platform events only
- Exact `outbox-publisher`, `platform-consumer`, and
  `internal-notification-consumer` principal-to-subject permission manifest;
  NATS credentials identify only those service roles
- Transactional Inbox consumers with idempotent, owner-scoped effects
- Internal notification event flow without Email, Push, webhook, or external
  delivery
- WAL archive incident state machine accepting synthetic age/failure samples
  and atomically producing the frozen internal Notification/AuditIntent pair;
  P1-006 owns only the collector, wiring, and integration proof
- Correlation-aware structured logging, metrics, and tracing
- Fault harness for duplicate, wrong-owner, reordered, disconnected, restarted,
  timed-out, ack-lost, stale-version, and poison messages

## Acceptance checks

- Redelivery, publisher restart, consumer restart, and lost acknowledgment
  produce one PostgreSQL business effect and one immutable audit chain.
- Before Inbox consumption or mutation, event user/account scope must match the
  target aggregate. The authenticated NATS service principal must be authorized
  for the subject and message type; it never supplies user/account authority.
  Wrong-owner delivery is rejected, audited, remains recoverable for the correct
  owner, and is never marked successfully consumed.
- Complete Phase 1 PostgreSQL Outbox replay rebuilds derived NATS state without
  inventing or dropping authoritative events.
- Aggregate versions reject stale and out-of-order mutations.
- Poison messages are bounded, quarantined, audited, and do not block healthy
  subjects indefinitely.
- Trace/log attributes follow the frozen allowlist, redact forbidden values,
  and use bounded metric labels.
- Metrics cover backlog, oldest event, retries, dead letters, wrong-owner
  rejects, duplicates, and reconciliation-required states.
- Subject-permission escalation, wrong service principal, wildcard overreach,
  user/account claims in credentials, and notification external-destination
  injection fail closed.
- The API has no NATS credential. Publisher subscribe, consumer publish,
  cross-consumer subject access, any `>`/`*`, and JetStream administration all
  fail.
- Synthetic WAL samples at 59/60/61 seconds, failure, repeats, clock movement,
  restart, and two-success recovery produce exactly the frozen incident
  transitions without needing archive infrastructure.
- Disposable PostgreSQL/NATS tests include process-kill and network-partition
  boundaries.

## Hard boundaries

- NATS is authenticated, internal, development-only, and default-disabled.
- Subjects and binaries contain no exchange connector, executor, signer, order
  submission, wallet, or external network capability.
- No deployment, production, or automatic trading.
- `MERGE/DEPLOY/PRODUCTION AUTHORIZED = NO`
