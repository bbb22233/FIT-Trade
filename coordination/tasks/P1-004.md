# P1-004 — NATS delivery, Inbox/Outbox, and observability

- Task ID: `P1-004`

- Base: exact accepted P1-003 commit

## Objective

Deliver durable internal events without turning NATS into an authoritative
database, and make duplicate, delayed, failed, and unknown delivery states
observable.

## Owned paths

- Allowed: `services/trading-core/**`, `infra/nats/**`,
  `infra/observability/**`
- Read-only: `contracts/**`, `docs/**`, `coordination/**`,
  `infra/postgres/**`, root `AGENTS.md`
- Forbidden: `services/hermes-agent/**`,
  `services/hyperliquid-adapter/**`, other `infra/**`

## Deliverables

- Outbox publisher with lease, retry, backoff, and crash recovery
- JetStream streams, subjects, consumers, retention, and dead-letter policy
- Transactional Inbox consumers with idempotent aggregate effects
- Correlation-aware structured logging, metrics, and tracing
- Fault-injection harness for redelivery, reordering, disconnect, restart,
  timeout, ack loss, and poison messages

## Acceptance checks

- NATS redelivery, publisher restart, consumer restart, and lost acknowledgments
  produce one PostgreSQL business effect.
- PostgreSQL Outbox can rebuild NATS delivery without inventing or dropping an
  authoritative event.
- Aggregate versions reject stale and out-of-order mutations.
- Poison messages are bounded, quarantined, audited, and do not block healthy
  subjects indefinitely.
- Trace/log attributes follow the frozen allowlist and redact forbidden values.
- Metrics expose backlog, oldest-event age, retries, dead letters, duplicates,
  and reconciliation-required states with bounded labels.
- Tests use disposable PostgreSQL and NATS instances and include process-kill
  boundaries.

## Hard boundaries

- NATS is internal, development-only, and default-disabled.
- No exchange subjects, live connector, executor, signer, wallet, deployment,
  or automatic trading.
- `MERGE/DEPLOY/PRODUCTION AUTHORIZED = NO`
